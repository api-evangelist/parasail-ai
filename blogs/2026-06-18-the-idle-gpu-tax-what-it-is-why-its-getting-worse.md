The idle GPU tax, also called the AI compute tax or model parking tax, is the power and cost you pay to keep a model loaded in GPU memory and ready to serve even when zero tokens are being processed. Parasail notes that average GPU utilization across the tech industry sits at just 5%, meaning roughly 95% is idle waste, and explains why this is getting worse and how to fix it.
